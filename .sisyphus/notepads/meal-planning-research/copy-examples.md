## TONE & VOICE GUIDELINES

### Tone:
- **Friendly, not corporate:** "Let's add your first recipe" not "Begin recipe importation"
- **Confident, not arrogant:** "Recipe chaos solved" not "The only recipe app you'll ever need"
- **Helpful, not pushy:** "Ready to upgrade?" not "You need Pro to do this"
- **Clear, not clever:** "Save recipes from Instagram" not "Capture culinary inspiration"

### Voice Characteristics:
- Direct and honest
- Empathetic to user pain points
- Focused on benefits, not features
- Conversational but professional
- Encouraging without being cheesy

### Words to Use:
- Your, you, yours (ownership)
- Simple, easy, quick (ease of use)
- Organize, collect, save (actions)
- Beautiful, clean, modern (design)
- Anywhere, everywhere (accessibility)

### Words to Avoid:
- Revolutionary, game-changing (hyperbole)
- Innovative, cutting-edge (buzzwords)
- Leverage, utilize (corporate speak)
- Discover, explore (implies browsing)
- Database, platform (technical jargon)
